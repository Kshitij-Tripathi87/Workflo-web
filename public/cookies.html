1. What the site currently uses

The current frontend does not set first-party HTTP cookies and does not load third-party analytics, advertising, retargeting or social tracking tags. It stores your privacy preference choice in browser local storage using the key workflo-privacy-consent-v1. Local storage is browser storage and is not itself an HTTP cookie.

StoragePurposeControl
workflo-privacy-consent-v1Remember your privacy preference selectionEssential site preference storage; can be cleared in browser settings

2. Optional categories

The privacy preferences interface shows functional, analytics and marketing categories so that future integrations can be governed deliberately. At present, no optional analytics or marketing scripts are installed, so selecting those categories does not activate a vendor or cause a tracker to load. This must remain true until a specific provider, purpose, script-blocking mechanism and privacy notice are reviewed and implemented.

3. Changing your selection

Use the “Privacy settings” control in the website footer to reopen the preference panel. You can reject optional categories, save a custom selection or accept all. You can also clear local storage through your browser controls; doing so removes the saved preference and the panel will be shown again on the next visit.

4. Future changes

If analytics, advertising or other optional technologies are introduced, Workflo must update this policy, describe each provider and purpose, prevent non-essential scripts from loading until the required permission has been obtained, and implement a reliable way to withdraw that permission. Do not add tags directly to the page without connecting them to the consent decision.
